What is a Wheebuy spreadsheet?

A Wheebuy spreadsheet is a product-discovery list that groups shopping links, often with category labels, prices, sizes or reference photos. Its usefulness depends on whether the links and product details are current. Use any list as a starting point, then verify the live listing before making a purchase.

Wheebuy shopping guide

A find is a lead.
Check what follows.

A useful Wheebuy finds spreadsheet makes discovery easier. Your final decision should come from current product information, clear measurements and a total cost you understand.

Keep a small comparison list with the product URL, selected variant, measurement notes and the date you last checked it.

  1. Choose a category and a purpose.

    Decide whether you need everyday shoes, an outer layer or a finishing accessory. A focused shortlist is easier to compare.

  2. Verify the current listing.

    Confirm the exact item, color, size, seller details and available purchase route. A spreadsheet entry may be older than the listing.

  3. Compare measurements and visible details.

    Use your own clothing measurements as a reference. If QC photos are available, check that they match the ordered variant.

  4. Calculate the complete cost.

    Include applicable delivery charges, service fees, currency conversion and taxes. Read the current return terms before paying.

Short answers to common questions about spreadsheet browsing, product checks and the links on this page.

What is a Wheebuy spreadsheet?

A Wheebuy spreadsheet is a product-discovery list that groups shopping links, often with category labels, prices, sizes or reference photos. Its usefulness depends on whether the links and product details are current.

Does this page provide a downloadable spreadsheet?

No. This page provides a category directory and a guide to checking finds. Its shopping links open CAFANS category pages or the CAFANS homepage.

How should I check a product link before ordering?

Open the current listing, confirm the exact variant, review measurements and seller information, and check the available return terms. Reference photos alone do not establish authenticity or current availability.

What should I compare in QC photos?

Compare the ordered color, size label, measurements, visible stitching, material appearance and included items against the listing. QC photos can reveal visible issues but cannot independently prove authenticity.

Is the displayed product price the final cost?

Not necessarily. Depending on the transaction, total cost may include domestic delivery, service fees, international shipping, currency conversion and applicable taxes or duties. Confirm the current charges before paying.

Is this an official Wheebuy or brand website?

No. This is an independent discovery guide and is not an official Wheebuy, Nike, Louis Vuitton, Gucci or Hermès website. Category illustrations do not confirm inventory, authenticity or an authorized seller relationship.
